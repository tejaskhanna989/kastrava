PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026. The code is public, so every claim below is checkable.

1. The short version

The browser sends zero telemetry — no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking of what you browse. Because the code is open and auditable, you never have to take our word for it: anyone can verify exactly what data is handled.

2. What the browser keeps (on your device only)

  • Bookmarks, settings, history and tabs live in a local database on your device.
  • Website data (cookies, cache, storage) lives in memory and is wiped when Kastrava exits.
  • Nothing you browse is sent anywhere unless the website itself sends it.

3. What our servers store (only with an account)

  • Login data: your email, a scrypt password hash (never the password), and salts for login and sync.
  • Sync blobs: encrypted bookmarks/settings only you can read — the server stores them but cannot decrypt them.
  • License data: your keys, their expiry, device names/IDs and app versions on each key, and purchase records.
  • Sessions and security log: active logins and a log of security events (logins, shares, approvals), visible on your dashboard.
  • Account emails: verification codes, login codes and receipts are emailed to you; sending runs through our mail relay.

4. What the apps send home

  • License checks: activation and update checks contact nexufog.pp.ua with your key, device ID and app version.
  • Update checks: the apps fetch a version file to learn about new releases.
  • That is the complete list. There is no analytics SDK, no advertiser ID collection, no browsing-history upload.

5. Payments

Card, UPI and netbanking payments are processed by Razorpay. Card numbers and bank credentials go to Razorpay, never to us — we only keep the order ID, amount, payment ID and the key it bought. Read Razorpay’s own privacy policy for their handling.

6. This website

These pages load no analytics trackers. The checkout loads Razorpay’s payment script, and pages use Google Fonts — that is all the third-party code here. The dashboard stores only your login token in the browser.

7. Your rights

Export or delete everything any time from the account dashboard (Your data card). Deleting wipes the account, sessions, sync copy and security log, and cancels owned keys. Anything else: ask us.
