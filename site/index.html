Answers

Straight answers.

What disappears when I quit?
Everything sites stored: cache, cookies, site data. It lives in RAM and is wiped on exit. Downloads, settings, bookmarks and history stay — unless you switch those off too.
Is the core really free?
Yes, forever. Premium ($19/month or $4.99/day) funds development. Everything else — sessions, blockers, downloads, Reader Mode, KastravaSearch, themes — costs nothing.
What does Premium unlock?
Tracking Radar, Cursor FX, Custom Theme Editor, Workspaces, Split View, Banking security guard, Local AI, Screenshot annotator, Anti-fingerprint, Tab power tools, Privacy autofill, E2E sync. One account, 10 devices.
How do renewals work?
Manually. You pay each time; nothing auto-charges. Paying again extends your same key, and every logged-in device picks it up.
How do I pay?
India: Razorpay in ₹ (UPI, cards, netbanking). Everywhere else: PayPal in your local currency. The page detects your location and shows the right rail.
I lost my key. Now what?
Email email-center@kastrava.in with your payment ID and we re-issue it.
Does activation spy on me?
No. One device ID plus your key, once. Browsing never leaves the machine, and the app works fully offline after activation.
Why "RC"?
Release candidate — still being built, rough edges exist. The privacy core already works.
Which platforms?
Linux (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, AppImage, Fedora), Windows (installer + portable) and Android (APK). No macOS.
Chromium-based?
Yes — same engine as Chrome, our own browser on top. None of their accounts or tracking.

Take your browser with you.

Linux, Windows and Android. Free forever — Premium unlocks inside the app.
