Questions

What exactly is deleted when I quit?

Everything websites stored: HTTP cache, cookies, DOM storage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches. They live in a private per-session sandbox and are wiped on every exit — even crash leftovers are purged at the next launch. Downloads are the exception: clicking download is explicit consent, so files save straight to your Downloads folder like any other browser. Your own settings, bookmarks and history database stay — unless you switch those off too.

Is the core really free? What's the catch?

Free forever, no accounts, no telemetry to monetize. Premium ($2.59 / 34 days, no auto-renew) is a one-time payment per cycle that powers development — but everything below is free: session isolation (website data never touches disk), blockers, tracking protection, downloads, Reader Mode, default keyboard shortcuts, KastravaSearch + DuckDuckGo, themes 1 light + 1 dark.

What does Premium actually unlock?

Tracking Radar (live per-tab privacy score), the full Cursor FX suite (all 8 effects + custom arrow), Workspaces, Split View, all 20 themes, extra search engines (Brave, Google, Ecosia), Picture-in-Picture, Screenshot, the Localhost Dashboard, bookmark import/export, vertical tabs, and custom shortcut remapping. One account, up to 10 devices. No auto-renew — the app reminds you when it's time to renew.

How does one account cover 10 devices?

Your key lives on your account, not on a machine. Log in inside the app and it activates itself — same key on every device, up to 10. The 11th needs a freed slot or a second key. Remove devices anytime from the account dashboard.

I changed PCs / lost my key. What now?

Email khannatejas989@gmail.com with your order payment ID. We verify and re-issue / unbind your key. We'd rather re-key you than argue.

Does activation phone home my browsing data?

No. Activation sends only a one-way hash of your hardware identifiers (a machine code) plus your license key — once. No history, no pages, no keystrokes, nothing you browse ever leaves the machine. And once activated, the app works even fully offline.

Why does it say RC?

Because it is. Kastrava (originally AnterSurf) is under active development — features land fast and rough edges exist. The privacy guarantees above are already enforced.

Which platforms are supported?

Linux (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, AppImage, Fedora/RHEL rpm), Windows (installer + portable, plus the Microsoft Store build) and Android (direct APK sideload; Play Store listing in progress). macOS builds are discontinued.

What search engine does it use?

KastravaSearch (kastravasearch.pp.ua) — Kastrava's own search — is the default. DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google and Ecosia are one click away in Settings.

Is Kastrava Chromium-based?

Yes. It is built on Chromium (Electron), with our own interface, privacy defaults and licensing on top. Same engine as Chrome and Edge, none of their accounts or telemetry.
